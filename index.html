SIH26191 Intelligent Decision Pipeline Architecture

This platform implements the complete end-to-end intelligent disaster management workflow mandated by SIH26191 for the Ministry of Home Affairs / National Disaster Management Authority:

1
Multi-Source Disaster Data Ingestion
CWC river gauges, IMD Doppler rainfall telemetry, ISRO Bhuvan DEM elevation, and Census 2021 demographic grids.
2
Uber H3 Hexagonal Spatial Indexing
Uniform discrete global grid representation (Res 7/8). Eliminates projection edge distortion and guarantees equidistant 6-neighbor spatial calculations.
3
Hazard & Multi-Factor Risk Assessment
Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) combining historical disaster frequency, elevation vulnerability, population density, and infrastructure fragility.
4
Population & Carrying Capacity Assessment
Evaluates safe local structural holding capacity during extreme events. Computes carrying capacity deficit: Deficit = PopAtRisk - SafeCapacity.
5
Evacuation Route & Priority Planning
Identifies vulnerable demographic pockets (infants, elderly, PwD). Dynamically routes around high-risk H3 cells and submerged road corridors.
6
Intelligent Safe Relocation Matching Engine
Multi-objective matching pairing critical Red Zone deficit cells with high-ground Green Zone safe cells based on capacity surplus, travel time, and medical proximity.
7
National Emergency Operations Command Center Dashboard
Real-time situational awareness, fleet dispatch orders, scenario simulation, and official automated SITREP generation for decision-makers.
