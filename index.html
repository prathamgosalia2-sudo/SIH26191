PRISM: Intelligent Disaster Decision Support Platform

Hazard-Based Red Zone Identification · H3 Carrying Capacity Assessment · Proactive Relocation Intelligence

Welcome to PRISM, an intelligent GIS-enabled command platform designed for the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA). It replaces reactive post-disaster evacuation with evidence-based, proactive relocation planning using discrete Uber H3 hexagonal spatial grids and multi-criteria mathematical modeling.

1. End-to-End Decision Workflow Pipeline
1
Multi-Source Disaster Telemetry Ingestion
Continuous sensor feeds: CWC river gauges (Teesta surge +6.2m at Chungthang, Rangeet surge +5.1m at Legship), IMD Doppler radar rainfall, ISRO Bhuvan DEM elevation, and Census 2021 demographic micro-grids.
2
Uber H3 Hexagonal Spatial Indexing (Res 7)
The watershed is tessellated into discrete H3 hexagonal cells. This eliminates edge projection distortions and guarantees equidistant 6-neighbor spatial calculations.
3
Multi-Criteria Dynamic Risk Scoring (MCDA)
Dynamically computes composite risk scores (0–100) using rainfall intensity, slope steepness, population density, infrastructure fragility, and road accessibility to classify Critical Red, Orange, Yellow, and Safe Green zones.
4
H3-Based Sustainable Carrying Capacity & Deficit
Calculates effective safe carrying capacity based on available land, water supply, healthcare, and road access. Computes exact population deficits (Deficit = Population − SafeCapacity) to prevent relocation site overcrowding.
5
3-Tier Relocation Prioritization
Categorizes habitations into: Immediate (<24h) for active Red Zone life threats; Short-Term (1–4 Weeks) for high-risk orange buffers; Medium-Term (3–12 Months) for permanent planned resettlement from chronic floodplains.
6
Intelligent Safe Relocation Matching Engine
Pairs deficit Red cells with resilient high-ground Green sanctuaries (Pelling High Ridge, Kabi Lungchok Sanctuary, Gyalshing District HQ Ridge) optimized for travel duration, surplus bed capacity, and hospital access.
7
National Emergency Operations Center & SITREP
Real-time tactical awareness, convoy dispatch coordination, scenario simulation scrubbing, and formal automated Situation Report (SITREP) exports.
